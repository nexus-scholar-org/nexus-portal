### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** What is the primary purpose of an ORCID iD?
- A) To track your h-index automatically.
- B) To provide a persistent digital identifier that distinguishes you from every other researcher, regardless of name changes or journal formatting.
- C) To host PDFs of your papers for free.
- D) To apply for academic jobs.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** According to the lesson, what is the main problem with using ResearchGate as your primary academic profile?
- A) It is too difficult to set up.
- B) It is a for-profit company that often encourages users to violate publisher copyrights by uploading final PDFs, and sends spam emails to colleagues.
- C) It is only used by biologists.
- D) It charges a monthly subscription fee.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — A personal website and Google Scholar are much safer, universally accepted alternatives.</details>

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** You are building a minimal academic personal website. Which of the following is the *least* important element to include?
- A) A list of your publications with links to open-access PDFs.
- B) A two-sentence bio explaining what you study.
- C) A blog with weekly 2,000-word updates on your lab work.
- D) A downloadable PDF of your CV.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** — A blog takes an immense amount of time to maintain. A digital business card (bio, papers, CV) requires almost zero maintenance.</details>

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** You published a paper in a journal, but it is not showing up on your Google Scholar profile. What is the most likely reason, and how do you fix it?

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>Google Scholar relies on automated crawling. If the journal is obscure or the metadata is poorly formatted, Scholar might miss it, or it might assign it to a different author with a similar name. To fix it, you must log into your Scholar profile, click "Add articles," search for the paper manually, and claim it.</details>

- C) Because it replaces the need for peer review.
