# Quiz: Module 5.1 — Presenting at Conferences

---

### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** According to the lesson, what is the primary purpose of presenting at an academic conference?
- A) To teach the audience every methodological detail of your study.
- B) To network, advertise your paper, and start conversations.
- C) To pass your PhD qualifying exam.
- D) To read your paper out loud to a room of people.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — A presentation is a trailer for the movie, not the movie itself.</details>

---

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** What is the core principle of the "Billboard" (or "Better Poster") approach to poster design?
- A) A massive, plain-English takeaway sentence in the center, minimal text, and a QR code.
- B) Using an 8-column layout to fit exactly 1,500 words.
- C) Including a copy of your abstract, introduction, methods, results, and discussion in full.
- D) Using complex, multi-layered 3D graphs to look impressive.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**A**</details>

---

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** You are giving a 10-minute talk. Which of the following is the best allocation of your time?
- A) 1 min Hook, 5 mins Methods, 3 mins Results, 1 min Conclusion.
- B) 1 min Hook, 2 mins Context, 5 mins Contribution/Results, 2 mins Takeaway.
- C) 5 mins Literature Review, 5 mins Results.
- D) 10 mins reading directly from your slides.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — Skip standard methods and focus on the contribution and takeaway.</details>

---

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** Read this elevator pitch: "I'm John. I use a modified U-Net architecture with a ResNet backbone and a custom loss function to segment MRI scans, achieving a Dice score of 0.89." Why is this a bad elevator pitch for a general conference coffee line?

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>It focuses entirely on the methods (U-Net, ResNet, loss function) and completely omits the **Problem** (why are we segmenting MRIs?) and the **Impact/Implication** (what does a 0.89 Dice score mean for patients or doctors?). It's too technical for a quick networking chat.</details>

---

### Q5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** A researcher argues: "I have to put all my text on the poster, because if I'm not standing there, people won't know what the study is about." How does the "Billboard" philosophy counter this?
- A) By arguing that no one reads posters when the presenter is absent anyway.
- B) By suggesting you leave a printed copy of your paper tacked to the board.
- C) By using a QR code that links directly to the full paper for anyone who wants the details.
- D) All of the above.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** (and practically A). The QR code solves the "absent presenter" problem much better than tiny, unreadable text.</details>
