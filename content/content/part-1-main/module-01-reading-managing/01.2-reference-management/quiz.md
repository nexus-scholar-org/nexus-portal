### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** What is the primary purpose of a Reference Manager?
- A) To find new papers for you to read.
- B) To separate bibliographic metadata from document formatting, allowing for automated citations and bibliography generation.
- C) To check your paper for plagiarism.
- D) To host your research on the internet.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** Why is Zotero often recommended over Mendeley for academic researchers?
- A) Mendeley cannot export to BibTeX.
- B) Zotero is open-source, highly customizable, and not tied to a for-profit publisher (Elsevier).
- C) Mendeley charges a massive upfront fee.
- D) Zotero is the only one that works on Windows.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** You are reading a paper on a publisher's website and want to add it to your library. What is the most efficient way to do this?
- A) Download the PDF, open your reference manager, click "Add New," and type in the title, author, and year manually.
- B) Copy the text of the citation, paste it into a Word document, and deal with it later.
- C) Click the Zotero/Mendeley browser extension button to automatically extract the metadata and download the PDF.
- D) Email the author and ask for the BibTeX file.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** — Never type metadata manually.</details>

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** Look at the following line of BibTeX code: `@book{Smith2024,`. What does `Smith2024` represent, and how is it used in the writing process?

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>`Smith2024` is the **citation key**. It acts as a unique identifier for that specific entry in the database. During the writing process, you use it as a placeholder (e.g., `\cite{Smith2024}`) to tell the software exactly which reference to format and insert at that location.</details>

### Q5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** A fellow student says they don't need a reference manager because they only have 10 citations in their paper, so typing them manually is fast enough. What is the strongest reason they should still use a reference manager?
- A) Manual typing is actually slower even for 10 citations.
- B) If the journal rejects the paper and they submit to a new journal with a different citation style, they will have to manually reformat all 10 citations, risking formatting errors.
- C) The university requires it for graduation.
- D) Reference managers automatically write the literature review for you.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>
