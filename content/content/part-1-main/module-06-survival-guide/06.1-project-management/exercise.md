# Exercise: The Kanban Breakdown
**Module:** 6.1 — Project Management for Researchers
**Estimated Time:** 20 minutes
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Intermediate

---

## Scenario
> You are starting a new research project: Writing a Systematic Literature Review. It feels overwhelming. You need to break it down into manageable Kanban tasks.

---

## Tasks

### Task 1: Deconstruct the Project
Break down "Write a Systematic Literature Review" into at least 8 discrete, actionable tasks. 
*(Hint: Think back to the PRISMA workflow from Module 3.4).*

### Task 2: Populate the Board
Assign your 8 tasks to the appropriate columns on a Kanban board for **Week 1** of the project.

- **Backlog:** (Things you will do eventually)
- **To Do:** (Things you commit to doing this week)
- **Doing:** (What you are working on *right now* - max 2 items)
- **Waiting:** (Things blocked by external factors)

### Task 3: Draft an Advisor Agenda
Draft a 4-part agenda for a meeting with your advisor to discuss the kickoff of this SLR project.
1. Progress:
2. Roadblock/Decision:
3. Proposed Solution:
4. Action Needed from Advisor:

### Task 1 & 2: Sample Board for Week 1
- **Doing:**
  - Define Research Question (PICO framework).
  - Draft inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- **To Do (This Week):**
  - Run initial search in OpenAlex to test query volume.
  - Setup Nexus RAG environment.
- **Backlog:**
  - Export PRISMA flow diagram.
  - Write Methods section.
  - Write Discussion section.
- **Waiting:**
  - (Nothing yet)

### Task 3: Sample Agenda
1. **Progress:** I have defined the research question and drafted the inclusion criteria for the SLR.
2. **Roadblock:** I am unsure if we should include preprints (arXiv) or restrict strictly to peer-reviewed journals.
3. **Proposed Solution:** Given how fast our field moves, I propose we include preprints but tag them for a separate sensitivity analysis.
4. **Action Needed:** I need your approval on the inclusion criteria so I can begin the full search protocol tomorrow.

*(Notice how this agenda makes the advisor's job incredibly easy? They just have to say "Yes, good idea.")*
