# Quiz: Module 6.1 — Project Management for Researchers

---

### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** In a standard Kanban board, what is the purpose of the "Waiting" column?
- A) For tasks you don't want to do.
- B) For tasks that are blocked by an external dependency (e.g., waiting for code to run, waiting for a co-author to review a draft).
- C) For tasks you have completed but haven't published yet.
- D) For tasks waiting to be added to the backlog.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

---

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** Why does the lesson recommend having a strict limit (e.g., maximum 2 items) in the "Doing" column?
- A) Because Trello charges money if you have more than 2 items.
- B) To prevent context-switching and cognitive overload, forcing you to finish a task before starting a new one.
- C) Because you shouldn't work more than 2 hours a day.
- D) Because the advisor only wants to hear about two things.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — Context-switching is the enemy of deep work.</details>

---

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** You have a meeting with your advisor. You are stuck on a bug in your code. What is the most effective way to present this on your meeting agenda?
- A) "I have a bug. Please help."
- B) Say nothing and hope they don't ask.
- C) "Roadblock: I am hitting an Out of Memory error. Proposed Solution: I researched it and think we need to batch the data processing. Action Needed: Can you approve paying $50 for a cloud computing instance to test this?"
- D) Cancel the meeting because you don't have good news.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** — Managing up requires bringing proposed solutions, not just problems.</details>

---

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** How does the Zettelkasten (atomic notes) method solve the "blank page" problem when writing a thesis?

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>Because you have been taking interconnected notes on individual concepts for years, when it comes time to write the thesis, you don't start from scratch. You simply assemble and edit the atomic notes you have already written.</details>

---

### Q5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** "Parkinson's Law" states that work expands to fill the time allotted for its completion. How does agile project management combat this in a PhD?
- A) By making the PhD take 6 years instead of 4.
- B) By breaking a massive 4-year project into strict 2-week "sprints," creating artificial, near-term deadlines that force focus.
- C) By forcing you to work on weekends.
- D) By giving you a grade every semester.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>
