**Task 1:** 
*Emotional:* "I am a terrible scientist. I should quit my PhD."
*Re-framed:* "The reviewer did not buy my justification for the small sample size. I need to either tone down the claims in the discussion, or explicitly justify the small sample size in the methods section for the next submission."

**Task 2:** Recognizing that you have failed many times and are *still here* is a powerful way to combat imposter syndrome. It proves resilience.

**Task 3:** This is the most important task. If an experiment fails on Friday, but you have a hiking trip planned for Saturday (Identity #1), the experiment failure is just a bad day at work, not a crisis of identity.
