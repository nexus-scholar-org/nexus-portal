## Summary for Portal
**Module 3.1: Critical Reading & Synthesis**
Your literature review is not a grocery list. It's a conversation. This lesson teaches the critical distinction between summary-based and synthesis-based literature reviews. You will learn to use extraction templates for consistent data collection, build synthesis matrices (concept matrices) that organize findings by theme rather than by paper, and apply the four synthesis moves (Convergence, Divergence, Gap, Evolution) to produce writing that your committee will approve on the first attempt.
