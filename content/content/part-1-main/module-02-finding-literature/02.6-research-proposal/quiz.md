### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** The Heilmeier Catechism was developed by George Heilmeier during his time as director of which organization?
- A) NASA
- B) NIH
- C) DARPA
- D) NSF

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** — DARPA (Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency)</details>

---

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** In the Logic Model, what is the difference between "Outputs" and "Impact"?
- A) There is no meaningful difference; they are the same thing.
- B) Outputs are the near-term, tangible deliverables (papers, datasets, software), while Impact is the long-term, real-world change that occurs years later.
- C) Outputs are what reviewers care about; Impact is irrelevant to grant applications.
- D) Impact refers to journal Impact Factor.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

---

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** A student writes this objective: "To improve healthcare outcomes using machine learning." Which criterion does it fail most severely?
- A) It is not Relevant.
- B) It is not Achievable.
- C) It is not Specific or Measurable — it has no defined population, method, time frame, or success metric.
- D) It is too long.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C**</details>

---

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** In a 5-section grant proposal, which section answers Heilmeier Questions 1, 2, and 4? What is the purpose of that section?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>The **Significance** section. Its purpose is to establish that the problem being addressed is real, important, and currently unsolved — convincing the reviewer that funding this research would matter to society.</details>

---

### Q5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** A reviewer reads a proposal and cannot trace a clear path from the requested budget to any measurable real-world benefit. According to the Logic Model, which element is missing?
- A) SMART objectives
- B) A clear Outcomes → Impact chain
- C) A budget justification
- D) A team section

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — The reviewer cannot see how the research activities will translate to meaningful outcomes and long-term impact.</details>
