# Exercise: Draft a Proposal Skeleton
**Module:** 2.6 — Writing a Research Proposal
**Estimated Time:** 25 minutes
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Intermediate

---

## Scenario
> Your supervisor has forwarded you a grant call from a national research council. The topic is open. The word limit is 2,000 words. The deadline is in 3 weeks. You need to decide what to propose and build the skeleton before you write a single word.

---

## Tasks

### Task 1: Answer the Heilmeier Catechism
Using your own research area (or the hypothetical scenario: *"Developing an AI-based early detection system for crop disease in smallholder farms"*), write 1–2 sentences answering each of the 7 Heilmeier questions:

1. What are you trying to do?
2. How is it done today, and what are the limits?
3. What is new in your approach?
4. Who cares? What difference does success make?
5. What are the risks and payoffs?
6. How much will it cost and how long will it take?
7. What are the mid-term and final exams for success?

---

**Task 1 (samples):**
1. *What:* "Build a smartphone-based AI tool that detects crop disease from leaf photos with 90% accuracy."
2. *Current limits:* "Farmers currently rely on agricultural extension officers who visit farms every 2–4 weeks — too slow to prevent spread."
3. *What's new:* "We use transfer learning on a novel dataset of 50,000 field-condition images from sub-Saharan Africa, unlike existing datasets captured in lab conditions."
4. *Who cares:* "250 million smallholder farmers in Africa lose 30–40% of yield to preventable disease annually."

**Task 3 (sample SMART objective):**
*"To train a convolutional neural network achieving ≥90% accuracy on leaf-disease classification across 5 major crop types, using a dataset of 50,000 annotated field images, within 12 months of project start."*
