## Summary for Portal
**Module 2.1: Designing Your Research Question**
A good research question is the difference between a thesis that takes three years and one that takes seven. This lesson teaches three frameworks (PICO, SPIDER, Heilmeier Catechism) for crafting precise, answerable research questions. You will learn to distinguish between a topic, a gap, and a question, perform systematic gap analysis using citation networks, and validate your question against the FINER criteria before committing years of work.
