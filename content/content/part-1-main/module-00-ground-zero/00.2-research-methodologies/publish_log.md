## Summary for Portal
**Module 0.2: Research Methodologies**
Before you can automate your research, you must understand how research is designed. This lesson demystifies the three core scientific paradigms: Quantitative (numbers and statistics), Qualitative (words and meaning), and Mixed Methods. You will learn why the biggest mistake junior researchers make is choosing their tool before defining their question, and how to evaluate your research design for both reliability (consistency) and validity (accuracy).
