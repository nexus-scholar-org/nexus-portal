## Summary for Portal
**Module 0.4: Predatory Publishing**
Protecting your reputation is just as important as publishing your research. In this lesson, we explore the dark side of academia: predatory publishing. You will learn how these scammers exploit the "publish or perish" culture, how to spot the red flags in those flattering email invitations, and how to use industry whitelists like the DOAJ to ensure your hard work lands in a legitimate, respected journal.
