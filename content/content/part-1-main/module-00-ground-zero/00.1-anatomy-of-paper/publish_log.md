**Module 0.1: Anatomy of a Research Paper**
This foundational lesson introduces the universal blueprint of empirical research: the IMRAD structure (Introduction, Methods, Results, And Discussion). You will learn why novices struggle by reading papers linearly like novels, and how to adopt the "Three-Pass Approach" to efficiently extract key information from dense academic texts in a fraction of the time.
