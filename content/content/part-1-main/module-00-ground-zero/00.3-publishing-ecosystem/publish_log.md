## Summary for Portal
**Module 0.3: The Publishing Ecosystem**
Science isn't science until it's shared. In this lesson, we demystify the black box of academic publishing. You will learn the lifecycle of a manuscript through the peer review process, the critical distinction between publishing in journals versus conferences (and how it impacts your career), and how to calculate and interpret academic metrics like the h-index and Impact Factor.
