# Lesson Specification: Module 4.1
## Metadata
| Field | Value |
|:---|:---|
| **Module** | Module 4.1 |
| **Title** | Academic Writing: Structure & Style |
| **Estimated Duration** | 30 minutes |
| **Prerequisites** | Module 3.1 (Critical Reading & Synthesis) |

## Learning Objectives
1. **Describe** the IMRaD structure and the specific question each section answers.
2. **Apply** the out-of-order writing strategy (Methods → Results → Discussion → Introduction).
3. **Demonstrate** hedging language, active voice, signposting, and the Paramedic Method.
4. **Construct** a reverse outline of a published paper to internalize structural blueprints.

## Sources
1. Swales, J. M. & Feak, C. B. (2012). *Academic Writing for Graduate Students*. University of Michigan Press.
2. Sword, H. (2012). *Stylish Academic Writing*. Harvard University Press.
3. Lanham, R. A. (2006). *Revising Prose* (The Paramedic Method). Pearson.
