# Quiz: Module 4.1 — Academic Writing: Structure & Style

---

### Question 1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** What does IMRaD stand for?
- A) Introduction, Methodology, Results, and Data
- B) Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion
- C) Information, Methods, Review, and Delivery
- D) Introduction, Model, Research, and Design

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

---

### Question 2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** According to this lesson, which section should you write *first*?
- A) Introduction
- B) Abstract
- C) Methods
- D) Discussion

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C** — Methods is the easiest to write because you already know what you did. The Introduction should be written last because it requires knowledge of the full story.</details>

---

### Question 3 (Apply)
**[Short Answer]** Rewrite this sentence using hedging language: "Our experiment proves that transformer models are superior to all other architectures."

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>"Our results suggest that transformer models outperformed the compared architectures in this specific evaluation context." — Note: "proves" → "suggest," "superior to all" → "outperformed the compared" (limits the scope).</details>

---

### Question 4 (Analyze)
**[Multiple Choice]** What is the "funnel structure" of an Introduction?
- A) Start with your results and work backwards to the question.
- B) Start with a broad field overview, narrow to the specific problem, identify the gap, and state your research question.
- C) Start with a personal anecdote and transition to the data.
- D) Start with the conclusion and explain how you arrived there.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**</details>

---

### Question 5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** Why does the lesson advise keeping sentences under 25 words?
- A) Because journals have a strict word limit per sentence.
- B) Because shorter sentences improve clarity, reduce ambiguity, and make complex ideas easier for the reader to process.
- C) Because longer sentences cause formatting errors.
- D) Because citation software cannot parse long sentences.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — The Paramedic Method is about ruthlessly cutting unnecessary words to reveal the core idea.</details>
