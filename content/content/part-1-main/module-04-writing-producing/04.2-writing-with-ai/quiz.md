### Q1 (Remember)
**[Multiple Choice]** According to the policies of major scientific publishers (e.g., Nature, Science), can an AI be listed as a co-author on a paper?
- A) Yes, if it generated more than 50% of the text.
- B) Yes, to ensure complete transparency.
- C) No, because an AI cannot take moral or legal responsibility for the work.
- D) Only if the AI is open-source.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C**</details>

---

### Q2 (Understand)
**[Multiple Choice]** What is an AI "hallucination" in the context of academic research?
- A) When the AI crashes due to too much data.
- B) When the AI generates a fake citation, author, or dataset that looks highly plausible but does not exist.
- C) When the AI copies text directly from Wikipedia.
- D) When the AI refuses to answer a prompt.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — LLMs predict patterns; they do not look up facts in a database.</details>

---

### Q3 (Apply)
**[Multiple Choice]** Which of the following is an *ethical* prompt to give an LLM when writing a paper?
- A) "Write a 300-word introduction about climate change impacts on coral reefs, with citations."
- B) "Here is my draft introduction. Please highlight any sentences that use passive voice and suggest active alternatives."
- C) "Generate a table of simulated patient data showing a positive response to the drug."
- D) "Summarize the findings of Smith (2022) so I can paste it into my literature review."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B** — This uses the AI as a structural editor for your own content, which is ethical and highly encouraged.</details>

---

### Q4 (Analyze)
**[Short Answer]** You used ChatGPT to fix grammatical errors in your manuscript. A reviewer finds an error where the AI accidentally changed the meaning of a key sentence, resulting in a false claim. Who is responsible, and why?

<details><summary>Sample Answer</summary>The human author is 100% responsible. By submitting the paper, the author takes full accountability for every word. "The AI made a mistake" is never a valid defense in academic publishing.</details>

---

### Q5 (Evaluate)
**[Multiple Choice]** Why is it important to include a transparency statement in your manuscript if you used AI to edit your writing?
- A) To avoid being charged with academic misconduct by proactively disclosing your methods.
- B) Because the AI company requires it for copyright reasons.
- C) So the reviewers will grade your paper more leniently.
- D) To prove that you know how to use modern technology.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**A** — Transparency protects the author and maintains the integrity of the scientific record.</details>
