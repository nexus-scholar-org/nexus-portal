# Exercise: Ethical AI Prompting
**Module:** 4.2 — Writing with AI: Ethics & Best Practices
**Estimated Time:** 20 minutes
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Intermediate

---

## Scenario
> You have written a rough, rambling first draft of a Discussion section paragraph. You want to use an LLM (like ChatGPT or Claude) to improve it, but you want to ensure the final product remains entirely your own intellectual property and complies with ethical guidelines.

## Draft Paragraph
*The results we got from the experiment show that the new algorithm is way faster than the old one, but it uses more memory. This is probably because of how we implemented the caching system, which stores a lot of redundant data arrays. Smith (2021) saw something similar in their work on memory leaks. We think this means our algorithm is good for high-speed stuff but not for edge devices with low RAM. However, we didn't test it on edge devices, so we can't be sure.*

---

## Tasks

### Task 1: Identify the Flaws
Before using AI, identify three issues with this draft based on the Academic Writing module (4.1). (Think about voice, hedging, and sentence structure).

### Task 2: Craft the Ethical Prompt
Write a prompt to feed to an LLM along with the draft paragraph. 
- **Rule:** You cannot ask the AI to "rewrite it to sound academic."
- **Goal:** Ask the AI to act as an editor, pointing out structural flaws, suggesting vocabulary upgrades, and asking you questions to clarify your meaning.

### Task 3: Draft the Transparency Statement
Write the exact 2-sentence disclosure statement you will put in your manuscript's Acknowledgments section regarding your use of the LLM for this paper.

### Task 1: Flaws
1. **Colloquial language:** "way faster", "high-speed stuff".
2. **Poor hedging:** "We think this means our algorithm is good..." (Should be more objective, e.g., "These results suggest...").
3. **Passive/Weak phrasing:** "The results we got from the experiment show..." (Active alternative: "Our experiment demonstrates...").

### Task 2: Ethical Prompt Example
*Prompt:* "Here is a rough draft of a paragraph for my Discussion section. I want to maintain my core argument, but the tone is too casual. Please act as a copyeditor. Do not rewrite the paragraph for me. Instead: 1) Point out colloquial phrases I should replace. 2) Suggest more precise academic verbs. 3) Help me format the final sentence as a formal acknowledgment of a limitation."

### Task 3: Transparency Statement Example
"During the preparation of this manuscript, the authors used ChatGPT (GPT-4) to assist with copyediting and improving the grammatical clarity of the text. The authors carefully reviewed all suggested edits and take full responsibility for the final content of the publication."
